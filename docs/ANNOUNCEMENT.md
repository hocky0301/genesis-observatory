# Announcement draft — not posted

GENESIS is an artificial-life observatory you can question.

Open an evolved world. Follow a lineage through its history. Predict a future
population, change one pressure, and compare the result with an untouched control.

Building Ecology Lab exposed something awkward: reading the old metrics changed
species bookkeeping, and sometimes the simulation itself. This edition
includes the failing source tests, the fixes, exact checkpoints, browser evidence,
and a film captured from the actual WebGL renderer with an original synthesized score.

Zero runtime dependencies. No build step. No claim that this is a real habitat,
lifetime learning, or a demonstration of general intelligence.

Film upload: none. This draft is kept as a record of what the release claims; it has not been posted anywhere.
